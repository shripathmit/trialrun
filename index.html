Questions, answered

The short version of everything people ask us.

Is TrialRun medical advice?

No. TrialRun helps you discover trials you may qualify for, but joining a study is a decision to make with your doctor. Always talk to your physician before enrolling in anything.

Where does the trial data come from?

Live recruiting studies straight from ClinicalTrials.gov, the US government's trial registry — the matcher queries it in real time. If the registry is unreachable, the demo falls back to a small sample dataset modeled on real public listings.

What does it cost patients?

Nothing, ever. Sponsors pay us per enrolled patient, which means our incentive is getting you into the right trial, not selling your data.

What happens to my health information?

In the live product, your details are used only for matching and pre-screening, never sold, and you can delete everything at any time. This demo stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
